# La Guerta Kuzina POS

## Run with XAMPP

1. Start **Apache** and **MySQL** in the XAMPP Control Panel.
2. Open `http://localhost/phpmyadmin/`.
3. For a new installation, select **Import**, choose this project's `schema.sql`, and select **Import**. This creates the `laguerta_pos` database, its tables, default 10% driver commission and 20% PWD discount rates, and the readable menu items/prices from the provided menu image.
   For an existing installation created from the earlier POS version, import `upgrade_reports_customers.sql`, then `upgrade_customer_orders.sql`, `upgrade_pwd_discount.sql`, `upgrade_deduction_rates.sql`, and `upgrade_public_customer_experience.sql` once each, in that order. The reports upgrade converts user accounts to customer accounts and adds expense/commission reporting; the customer-orders upgrade adds Food/Drinks classification and the order queue; the PWD upgrade adds the sale discount field; the deduction-rates upgrade adds rate snapshots and records historical driver commissions as expenses; the public customer experience upgrade adds guest contact numbers and customer feedback. Do not run an upgrade file more than once.
4. Open `http://localhost/POS/setup.php` and create the first administrator. There are no built-in usernames or passwords.
5. Open `http://localhost/POS/` to explore the public customer menu. Admin and cashier sign-in links are available in the navigation bar.
6. As admin, create cashier accounts from the dashboard. Customers can browse and add items without signing in; at checkout they provide their name and contact number, and an account/session is created automatically so they can track their orders. Customers can also rate and review the restaurant or website; reviews appear immediately, and admins can hide or republish them from **Ratings & reviews**. Dashboard sections are organized into tabs. The Overview opens on today's date by default and automatically rolls over at midnight Philippines time, while Sales history and the date selector let you view past days without deleting them. Completed sales are retained in the database indefinitely unless someone manually removes them. The dashboard also lets admins record expenses and adjust driver commission and PWD discount rates from 0% to 100%. Driver commission is calculated from the amount due after any PWD discount when a cashier enters a driver name; each sale keeps the rates used at checkout.
7. Cashiers can apply the configured PWD discount at checkout after verifying the PWD ID. The discount is saved with the sale, shown with its applied rate on the receipt and sales report, and automatically recorded in Expenses under **PWD Discount**. Driver commissions are also automatically recorded in Expenses under **Driver Commission** with their applied rates. Both deductions are included in expenses and are not subtracted a second time in net sales.
8. Customers can add food and drinks, submit dine-in/takeout orders, or book advance orders/catering with a date, time, and headcount. They can add allergy/special-request notes, see estimated totals, and track their order. Cashiers and admins use **Customer orders** to review notes, confirm orders, update progress, and complete them; they can apply a PWD discount while completing an order after verifying the ID. Completed customer orders are saved into sales and get a receipt. Cashiers can also create counter orders from the POS.
9. Admin and cashier pages check for new customer orders every 10 seconds. New orders show a notification and pending-order count in the navbar; the Customer orders page refreshes automatically so the new order appears.

The database connection defaults to XAMPP's local MySQL settings (`127.0.0.1`, database `laguerta_pos`, user `root`, empty password). The app uses Philippines time (Asia/Manila, UTC+08:00) for daily reporting. Override the database connection with `POS_DB_HOST`, `POS_DB_NAME`, `POS_DB_USER`, and `POS_DB_PASSWORD` environment variables if your MySQL credentials differ.

Passwords are stored using PHP's password hashing. Sale totals, commission, and line items are calculated and saved by the server, not trusted from browser input. Cashiers cannot access admin reports or other cashiers' receipts. The provided menu photo appears at the top of the customer menu. All existing seeded menu items are classified as Food; admins can add drinks and choose Food or Drinks in the menu-management section. Items whose prices could not be read clearly from the photo are not seeded; add them from the admin dashboard when their prices are confirmed.
